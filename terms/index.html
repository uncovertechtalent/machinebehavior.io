    Canonical definitions, frozen wording

    Terms

    The program's vocabulary, one definition each. Wording here is canonical; posts and papers quote it verbatim.

    Layered symptom substitution
    Sycophancy is layered symptom substitution: suppress the reflex at one layer and it resurfaces in the next. The layers run lexical (word choice), stance (behavior under correction and push), and premise (uncritical adoption of the user's frame).
    Receipt: the paper, and the founding specimen's month-by-month suppression log.
    The fawn machine
    The output stance produced by approval training: appease the counterpart, avoid the rupture, cushion everything. A fawn response at industrial scale, arriving as the default register of every approval-trained model.
    Receipt: the fawn-machine case file (redd.it/1vh56s1).
    Vestige
    A trained output pattern that survives instruction-level suppression: praise reflexes, hedge phrases, service-worker closers, consensus reflexes, and their relatives. Vestiges are operator-specific and get catalogued, then enforced against mechanically.
    Receipt: vestige-kit, including the calibration procedure that derives a catalog.
    Waste gate
    A mechanical valve at the output boundary that diverts trained excess (hedging, cushioning, praise) before it reaches the reader, paired with a ledger that meters what got diverted. Diversion without metering is just deletion; the meter is where the findings come from.
    Receipt: the waste-gate post (redd.it/1vi7ddg). 100 turns blocked for one pattern in 34 days; the post quotes 297, the weighted count the site retired on 2026-09-22.
    Behavioral momentum
    The tendency of a model to imitate its own recent in-context output. Proposed carrier of runtime compliance: a filter would hold mid-session because the context fills with examples of corrected output, and fail at cold starts because it has not. Proposed, not established.
    Receipt: the half-life study on the experiments page. Status: open. The exemplar-seeding test showed no change at its dose (2026-09-22, scored by blocked-turn event); three earlier attributions were withdrawn as a metric artifact; the mechanism is open pending the good-only arm.
    Cold start
    The fresh-context regime: session open, post-resume, or post-compaction, where the context holds no recent history of the model's own corrected output. Where relapse concentrates, at roughly 5x the mid-session rate per unit of prose by blocked-turn event (7x by the older weighted count).
    Receipt: regime table, experiments page.
    Working-man's ontology
    An informal typed knowledge graph in plain markdown: atomic notes as entities, wikilinks as relations, frontmatter as the class system, index notes as hierarchy. Trades formal reasoning for a graph both human and agent can write to all day without ceremony.
    Receipt: vault-kit, and a P0 production incident resolved in 13 minutes over one.
    Earned-secure model
    The target state: a model that takes correction as information, holds positions under push, changes them on evidence, and states confidence inside the claim. Named for the attachment profile of the same shape. The program's aim is that behavioral specs upstream produce this at training time, so the fawn bias never enters.
    Receipt: the stance spec and its relapse log, fawn-machine case file.
