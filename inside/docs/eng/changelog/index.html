    What changed on machinebehavior.io and the platform behind it, newest first: 25 closed issues and 137 commits since 2026-08-07, grouped by day into Added, Changed and Fixed. Generated by scripts/build_changelog.py from the closed issues and the commit history; the conformity bot's commits and map snapshot refreshes are left out. Decisions behind the changes: Decision records.

    How an entry is sorted. A closed issue goes by its type label: feature to Added, bug to Fixed, docs or chore to Changed. A commit goes to Fixed when its subject says fix, repair, correct or restore, to Added when it says add or new or names a new page path, and to Changed otherwise. The rule reads words, so a few commits land in the wrong group.
