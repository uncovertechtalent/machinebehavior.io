    COBIT 2019 Cluster

    Map of ISACA COBIT 2019 — IT governance framework. Governance vs management distinction. 40 governance and management objectives. Companion to ITIL (governance vs operations).

    Anchors

    • position · anchors

    Provenance

    • COBIT 1 (1996) — ISACA's IT audit framework.
    • COBIT 4.0 / 4.1 (2005 / 2007) — broader IT governance.
    • COBIT 5 (2012) — major restructure; governance vs management; enterprise IT.
    • COBIT 2019 — current. Renamed (dropped version number). Updated design factors, focus areas concept.

    What COBIT is

    Framework for IT governance and management. Distinguishes:

    • Governance (board-level): ensure stakeholder needs, conditions, options evaluated; direction set; performance measured.
    • Management (executive-level): plan, build, run, monitor activities aligned with governance direction.

    40 governance and management objectives organized in five domains.

    Five domains

    • EDM (Evaluate, Direct, Monitor) — governance domain, 5 objectives.
    • APO (Align, Plan, Organize) — management, 14 objectives.
    • BAI (Build, Acquire, Implement) — management, 11 objectives.
    • DSS (Deliver, Service, Support) — management, 6 objectives.
    • MEA (Monitor, Evaluate, Assess) — management, 4 objectives.

    Per objective: practices, activities, work products, capability levels.

    Detail in COBIT Governance and Management Objectives.

    Design factors

    COBIT 2019 introduced design factors for tailoring:

    • Enterprise strategy
    • Enterprise goals
    • Risk profile
    • I&T-related issues
    • Threat landscape
    • Compliance requirements
    • IT sourcing
    • IT implementation methods
    • Technology strategy
    • Enterprise size
    • Role of IT

    Design factors produce tailored governance system.

    Focus areas

    COBIT 2019 publishes focus-area guides for specific topics:

    • Information security
    • DevOps
    • SMB
    • Risk
    • Information & technology risk
    • Digital transformation
    • Others

    Focus areas extend COBIT for specific contexts.

    COBIT vs ITIL

    • COBIT: IT governance — what should IT achieve.
    • ITIL: IT service management — how IT delivers services.
    • Complementary, not competing.
    • Many orgs use both.

    Certification

    ISACA certifications:

    • COBIT 2019 Foundation
    • COBIT 2019 Design and Implementation
    • Additional advanced certifications.

    Also CRISC, CISM, CISA, CGEIT — broader ISACA certifications often combined.

    Why this matters

    • IT governance vocabulary widely used.
    • Board-level IT oversight framing.
    • Combined with ITIL for full IT-operations coverage.
    • US enterprise + financial-services use.
