WHY THESE NUMBERS

The term is an empty query on 2026-10-09. An answer engine that retrieves from a live web index returns the only pages that define an empty term, so the first clear definition has a strong position. That is the mechanism under test, and it is why the bar for P1 is 2 engines and not 3: Google shows AI Overviews for some queries and not others, and a new low-volume phrase may get none. 2026-11-30 is seven weeks after publication, which covers several crawl cycles for a site that publishes a sitemap and allows every crawler in robots.txt.

P3 is the control. "fractional CTO Germany" is a contested query with established providers. If the method only fills empty queries, P3 holds. If P3 fails and an engine names him there, that is a result to report and explain, and it does not count for P1.

WHAT REFUTES IT

P1 is refuted if fewer than 2 engines name Stefan Coetzee for the term on 2026-11-30. P2 is refuted if no engine cites a machinebehavior.io URL for it. The result goes on the experiment page with the probe log, whichever way it lands.

WHAT THE EXPERIMENT DOES NOT DO

No paid press releases, no invented events, no exact-match domains, no hidden text, no text addressed to models. The inputs are the pages under /your-next-cto/, the article "Who should your next CTO be?" once it is published, and links Stefan adds from his own profiles.
