    Conformity and law

    Continuous conformity for deployed AI systems, the gate that checks this site on every push, and the standards and regulations the requirements map to, from the EU AI Act to ISO 42001.

    Inside 2

    • Gate runs

      Continuous conformity of machinebehavior.io, site tier: the mechanical requirements of the working draft checked on every push and every week, with run records, open findings and the crosswalk to existing frameworks. Self-assessment, not a certification.

      updated 2026-10-07
    • Conformity gate

      The checks every push runs before a site serves readers: blocking rule hits, placeholder text, prediction hashes and site consistency. A failed check stops the deploy and the previous build stays live. One composite action, shared by the three sites.

      updated 2026-10-09
