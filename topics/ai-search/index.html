    AI search

    How the engines behind AI answers pick the pages they cite, how this site is built for them, and how citations get measured.

    1 research page, 4 Inside pages, 1 docs page, 1 ticket

    Research 1

    • Terms

      Canonical definitions for the Machine Behavior program's vocabulary: layered symptom substitution, the fawn machine, vestige, waste gate, behavioral momentum, cold start, working-man's ontology, earned-secure model.

      updated 2026-10-07

    Inside 4

    • AIEO

      AI engine optimisation (AIEO, also called AEO or GEO): what Google, Bing, OpenAI, Perplexity and Anthropic document about which pages their AI answers retrieve and cite, quoted from their own pages, and the boundary this site keeps.

      updated 2026-10-09
    • This site

      Each AI search lever on machinebehavior.io as a receipt: what the site does, which engine documents reading it (or no primary source), and where to check it. Crawl access, sitemap, canonicals, IndexNow, static HTML, JSON-LD, llms.txt, the link graph, Open Graph images and answer-first pages.

      updated 2026-10-09
    • Measurement

      How AI search citations of machinebehavior.io can be measured: the Search Console Generative AI performance report, Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance and a Perplexity API probe. What each one counts, what it leaves out, and the status of each on this site. No baseline is published until a run exists.

      updated 2026-10-09
    • Map

      Stefan Coetzee's published work as a graph: every page on machinebehavior.io, tychat.io and uncovertechtalent.com, the Substack posts, repos and threads, and the links between them.

      updated 2026-10-08
