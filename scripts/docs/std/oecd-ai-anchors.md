## Primary documents

- **OECD Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence** (May 2019, revised May 2024). Free from OECD.
- **OECD AI Policy Observatory publications** — country profiles, thematic reports.
- **OECD Framework for the Classification of AI Systems**.

## Operating bodies

- **OECD** — Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development. 38 member countries.
- **OECD Working Party on Artificial Intelligence Governance** (AIGO).
- **OECD AI Policy Observatory** — oecd.ai.
- **Global Partnership on AI (GPAI)** — partner forum.

## Adherent jurisdictions

OECD members (38) + non-member adherents (Argentina, Brazil, Costa Rica, Egypt, Malta, Peru, Romania, Singapore, Ukraine, and growing — check oecd.ai for current).

## Related instruments

- **G20 AI Principles** (2019) — drew on OECD.
- **G7 Hiroshima AI Process** (2023) — voluntary code of conduct.
- **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI** (2021).
- **Bletchley Declaration** (November 2023) — AI Safety Summit.

## Implementation frameworks aligned

- **NIST AI RMF** (US)
- **ISO/IEC 42001** (international)
- **EU AI Act** (regulatory)
- **Singapore Model AI Governance Framework + AI Verify**
- **UK AI Action Plan**
- Various national strategies
