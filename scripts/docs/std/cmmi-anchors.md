## Primary documents

- **CMMI v3.0** (2023) — current model.
- **CMMI v2.0** (2018) — predecessor; still in use.
- **CMMI for Development**, **CMMI for Services**, **CMMI for Supplier Management** — domain views.

## Operating body

- **ISACA / CMMI Institute** — owner since 2017 acquisition from CMU SEI.
- **CMU SEI** (Software Engineering Institute) — historical owner.

## Appraisal community

- **Certified Lead Appraisers** worldwide.
- **CMMI Partner Network** — accredited training and appraisal firms.

## Adjacent frameworks

- **ASPICE** — automotive sibling.
- **ISO/IEC 33000 (SPICE)** — ISO process assessment.
- **ITIL** — service management overlap.
- **PMBOK** — project management overlap.
- **Agile / Scrum** — methodology complement (CMMI 3.0 agile-friendly).
