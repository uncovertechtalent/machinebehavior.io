## Bureaucratic reputation

CMM heritage produces bureaucratic-implementation perception:

- "CMMI Level 3" often documentation-heavy.
- Process discipline can become process ceremony.
- DevOps / agile practitioners often skeptical.

## Implementation cost

Levels 3+ require substantial investment:

- Process documentation.
- Training.
- Internal audit infrastructure.
- Appraisal fees.
- Ongoing maintenance.

Smaller orgs disproportionately burdened.

## Procurement signal narrow

US DoD-adjacent strong; broader procurement weak:

- EU procurement rarely references CMMI.
- Tech-native orgs typically don't ask.
- Indian / offshore services-export-driven.

## Agile / DevOps tension

Historical CMMI predates agile:

- v3.0 addresses but tension remains.
- Pure-agile orgs find CMMI rigid.
- Hybrid implementations sometimes awkward.

## ISACA acquisition trajectory

Since 2017 ISACA ownership:

- Direction depends on ISACA priorities.
- Some practitioners concerned about commercialization.
- Cost dynamics changing.

## Counterpoint

- Genuine process maturity framework.
- Defense and aerospace recognition real.
- v3.0 modernizations material.
- Capability-level approach (vs maturity) flexible.
