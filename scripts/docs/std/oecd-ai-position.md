# OECD AI Principles Position

## What they do well

- **First international AI standard** — diplomatic baseline established.
- **Values-based principles** durable across AI technology evolution.
- **Brussels-effect catalyst** — many subsequent frameworks aligned.
- **2024 update** addresses GenAI maturation.
- **OECD AI Policy Observatory** as ongoing tracking infrastructure.

## What they do poorly

- **Voluntary only.** No enforcement.
- **High abstraction.** Operational implementation requires concrete frameworks (NIST, ISO).
- **Adherent state variance.** Implementation differs across adherent countries.
- **Limited operational specificity.**

## Evidence

- 50+ jurisdictions adherent.
- Referenced in NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, EU AI Act, multiple national strategies.
- OECD AI Policy Observatory tracks national implementations.
- 2024 update keeping current.

## Personal calibration

- For international vendor positioning: OECD AI alignment statement defensible signal.
- For operational implementation: NIST AI RMF + ISO 42001 + OWASP LLM Top 10 provide depth.
