## Document-heavy

Management products + registers + reports:

- Substantial documentation burden.
- Ritual completion vs substantive use.
- Smaller-project disproportionate overhead.

## Tailoring discipline weak

Principle 7 (tailor to suit) often ignored in practice:

- Full method applied regardless of context.
- Cargo-cult implementation.

## Agile tension

PRINCE2's stage-gate structure friction with Agile iterative delivery:

- PRINCE2 Agile addresses but doesn't eliminate.
- Hybrid approaches sometimes awkward.

## CPD renewal cost (2023+)

PeopleCert added CPD renewal:

- Past lifetime-certification holders now face renewal.
- Cost over time.
- Some practitioner backlash.

## US PMBOK dominance

PRINCE2 less recognized in US market:

- PMP from PMI dominates US.
- Cross-Atlantic practitioner navigation cost.

## Bureaucratic perception

UK government lineage produces bureaucratic-implementation reputation:

- Sometimes deserved.
- Modern PRINCE2 7 addresses but reputation persists.

## Counterpoint

- Genuinely useful method for governance-heavy projects.
- Principles solid and durable.
- Tailoring guidance present (when followed).
- UK / EU public-sector recognition real procurement signal.
