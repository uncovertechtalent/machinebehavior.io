> Map of ISO/IEC 22301:2019 — Business Continuity Management Systems. Annex SL sibling to ISO 27001 + ISO 42001. Certifiable. Used alongside ISO 27001 + DORA for orgs with continuity obligations.

## Anchors

- [position](pillars/iso-22301/position.md) · [anchors](pillars/iso-22301/anchors.md)

## Provenance

- **BS 25999** (UK origin, 2006-2007) — first BC management standard.
- **ISO 22301:2012** — first ISO version.
- **ISO 22301:2019** — current. Annex SL-aligned.

## What ISO 22301 is

Management system standard for business continuity. Defines requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, continually improving a BCMS. Covers planning, preparing for, responding to, recovering from disruption.

## Clause structure (Annex SL)

Mandatory clauses 4-10, identical structural pattern to ISO 27001:

- Cl 4 Context
- Cl 5 Leadership
- Cl 6 Planning
- Cl 7 Support
- Cl 8 Operation (incl. business impact analysis, risk assessment, BC strategy, BC plans, exercises, evaluation)
- Cl 9 Performance Evaluation
- Cl 10 Improvement

Detail in [[ISO 22301 Clause Structure and Key Concepts]].

## Key concepts

- **Business Impact Analysis (BIA)**: identify what processes / activities are time-critical, quantify maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD), recovery time objective (RTO), minimum business continuity objective (MBCO).
- **Risk Assessment**: identify risks of disruption.
- **BC Strategy**: how disruption will be addressed.
- **BC Plans**: incident response plans, business recovery plans.
- **Exercises and Testing**: tested plans.
- **Evaluation**: monitor effectiveness.

## Certification mechanics

Same Annex SL pattern as ISO 27001:

- Stage 1 + Stage 2 initial audit.
- 3-year certificate.
- Annual surveillance audits.
- Recertification at 3-year mark.

## Why this matters for SRE and AI-agent work

- **Sibling to ISO 27001** under Annex SL — combined ISMS + BCMS implementation common.
- **DORA business continuity** obligations (Pillar 1) align with ISO 22301.
- **NIS2 Article 21(c)** business continuity overlaps.
- **AI-feature continuity**: model-vendor outages, AI-service unavailability, fallback strategies.
