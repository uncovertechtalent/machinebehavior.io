## Primary documents

- **AICPA Trust Services Criteria (2017, revised 2022)** — full TSC document.
- **SSAE 18** — current attestation standard.
- **AT-C section 105** — concepts common to all attestation engagements.
- **AT-C section 205** — assertion-based examinations (Type 2).
- **AICPA SOC 2 Reporting Guide** — implementation reference for auditors and service organizations.

## Operating bodies

- **AICPA** (American Institute of Certified Public Accountants) — owner of attestation standards.
- **AICPA Auditing Standards Board (ASB)** — develops standards.
- **State boards of accountancy** — regulate CPA practice in US states.

## Common audit firms

CPA firms with SOC 2 capability:

- **Schellman** — large SOC 2 + ISO 27001 + ISO 42001 + HITRUST volume.
- **A-LIGN** — similar positioning.
- **Coalfire** — security-specialist CPA firm.
- **BSI Americas** — UK-headquartered, US operations for SOC 2.
- **Deloitte, KPMG, PwC, EY** — Big Four with SOC 2 practices.
- **BPM, BPM SOC, Many others** — regional / mid-market.

## Adjacent frameworks

- **ISO 27001:2022** — international sibling. Combined audits common.
- **HITRUST CSF** — healthcare-broadened US framework.
- **PCI DSS** — payment card industry.
- **HIPAA Security Rule** — US healthcare.
- **FedRAMP** — US federal cloud.
- **NIST CSF 2.0** — voluntary US framework.
