## 1. Inclusive growth, sustainable development, well-being

AI should contribute to inclusive growth, sustainable development, well-being for people and planet:

- **Inclusive growth**: AI should reduce inequalities, not amplify.
- **Sustainable development**: AI should support SDGs.
- **Well-being**: AI should advance human well-being broadly defined.

2024 update emphasized sustainability dimension.

## 2. Human-centred values and fairness

AI should respect:

- Rule of law
- Human rights
- Democratic values

Including (non-exhaustive):

- Freedom
- Dignity
- Autonomy
- Privacy and data protection
- Non-discrimination and equality
- Diversity
- Fairness
- Social justice
- Internationally recognised labour rights

Mechanisms include human oversight, particularly for high-stakes AI.

## 3. Transparency and explainability

AI should be transparent and explainable:

- **Information about AI systems**: those affected should know AI is involved.
- **Explainability**: outcomes should be understandable.
- **Documentation**: AI systems' purposes, capabilities, limitations documented.
- **Information for affected parties**: appropriate to outcomes and stakes.

## 4. Robustness, security and safety

AI should be robust, secure, safe throughout lifecycle:

- **Reliability**: consistent operation as intended.
- **Security**: resistance to adversarial attack.
- **Safety**: no unreasonable risk.
- **Traceability**: ability to audit decisions.

2024 update strengthened with information integrity (countering mis/disinformation).

## 5. Accountability

AI actors should be accountable:

- **Responsibility for proper functioning**.
- **Risk management** throughout lifecycle.
- **Adherence to other principles**.
- **Cooperation with regulators**.
- **Pathways for affected parties to seek redress**.

## Connection to operational frameworks

Each principle implemented operationally via:

- **Inclusive growth / sustainable development**: ISO 42001 impact assessment Annex A.5; NIST AI RMF GenAI Profile Category 5 (environmental) + Category 6 (bias).
- **Human-centred values**: ISO 42001 A.5; EU AI Act Article 26 (deployer obligations) + Annex IV; GDPR.
- **Transparency**: ISO 42001 A.8; EU AI Act Article 50 + Article 13; NIST AI RMF.
- **Robustness, security, safety**: OWASP LLM Top 10; MITRE ATLAS; ISO 42001 A.6; EU AI Act Article 15.
- **Accountability**: ISO 42001 Cl 5; NIST AI RMF GOVERN function; ISO 27001 Cl 5.
