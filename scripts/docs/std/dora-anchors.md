## Primary text

- **Regulation (EU) 2022/2554** — DORA. Published OJEU 27 December 2022. Applicable 17 January 2025.
- **Directive (EU) 2022/2556** — companion directive amending sector-specific regulations to enable DORA application.
- **Recitals** — 106 recitals.
- **Articles** — 64 articles.

## RTS / ITS publications (selected)

ESAs published numerous Regulatory Technical Standards and Implementing Technical Standards specifying technical and operational requirements. Examples:

- **RTS on ICT risk management framework**
- **RTS on classification of major ICT-related incidents**
- **ITS on reporting of major ICT-related incidents**
- **RTS on TLPT — threat-led penetration testing**
- **RTS on policy on the use of ICT services supporting critical or important functions**
- **RTS on register of information**
- **RTS on subcontracting of ICT services**
- **RTS on harmonization of conditions enabling the conduct of oversight activities**
- **ITS on register of information**

## ESAs (Joint Committee for DORA)

- **EBA** (European Banking Authority) — eba.europa.eu
- **ESMA** (European Securities and Markets Authority) — esma.europa.eu
- **EIOPA** (European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority) — eiopa.europa.eu

Joint Committee coordinates cross-ESA work.

## National competent authorities (DE example)

- **BaFin** (Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht) — banking, insurance, securities.
- **Bundesbank** — central bank cooperation.

## Related instruments

- **TIBER-EU** — Threat Intelligence-Based Ethical Red Teaming framework. Foundation for DORA TLPT.
- **EBA Guidelines on ICT and Security Risk Management** — predecessor for banking.
- **EBA Outsourcing Guidelines** — predecessor for outsourcing arrangements.

## Adjacent EU legislation

- **NIS2 (Dir 2022/2555)** — lex generalis; DORA prevails for financial entities.
- **MiCA (Reg 2023/1114)** — Markets in Crypto-Assets. Applies alongside DORA for CASPs.
- **GDPR** — co-applies.
- **EU AI Act** — AI features in financial entities co-apply.
