> Map of PRINCE2 (PRojects IN Controlled Environments). UK-origin project management methodology. Owned by PeopleCert (sibling to ITIL since 2021 acquisition). PRINCE2 7 (2023) current version. Heavily used in UK / EU public sector and many private orgs.

## Anchors

- [position](pillars/prince2/position.md) · [anchors](pillars/prince2/anchors.md)

## Provenance

- **PROMPT** (1975) — first project management methodology by Simpact Systems.
- **PRINCE** (1989) — UK CCTA adoption for IT projects.
- **PRINCE2** (1996) — generalized for all project types.
- **PRINCE2 2009** — major revision.
- **PRINCE2 2017** — revision.
- **PRINCE2 7** (2023) — current.
- **PeopleCert acquired AXELOS 2021** — current owner.

## What PRINCE2 is

Process-based project management methodology. Structured approach with principles, themes, processes. Tailoring for project context.

## PRINCE2 7 structure

### Seven principles

1. Continued business justification
2. Learn from experience
3. Defined roles, responsibilities, and relationships
4. Manage by stages
5. Manage by exception
6. Focus on products
7. Tailor to suit the project

### Seven aspects (PRINCE2 7 — previously "themes")

1. Business case
2. Organisation
3. Plans
4. Risk
5. Issues
6. Progress
7. Quality

(PRINCE2 6 had "themes"; PRINCE2 7 renames to "aspects.")

### Seven processes

1. Starting up a project
2. Directing a project
3. Initiating a project
4. Controlling a stage
5. Managing product delivery
6. Managing a stage boundary
7. Closing a project

Detail in [[PRINCE2 Principles Aspects Processes]].

## PRINCE2 7 changes from PRINCE2 6 (2017)

- **People** dimension elevated.
- **Sustainability** added as aspect.
- **Digital and data** aware.
- **Hybrid / Agile** approach support improved.
- **Themes renamed aspects**.
- **Roles refined**.

## Certification

PeopleCert certifications:

- **PRINCE2 Foundation**
- **PRINCE2 Practitioner**
- **PRINCE2 7 versions of both**
- **PRINCE2 Agile Foundation / Practitioner** (combines with Agile)

CPD-renewal required since 2023 (similar to ITIL).

## PRINCE2 vs PMBOK / PMI

- **PRINCE2**: UK-origin, principles + processes, prescriptive structure with tailoring.
- **PMBOK (PMI)**: US-origin, process areas + knowledge areas, more practitioner-discretion-driven.
- Different conceptual approaches; complementary.
- PMI offers PMP certification.
- Many practitioners hold both.

## Why this matters

- UK public sector + EU public sector frequently require PRINCE2.
- Private sector adoption in UK / EU.
- PeopleCert sibling positioning with ITIL.
- DE / Mittelstand: some adoption.
