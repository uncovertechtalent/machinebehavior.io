# OECD AI Principles Cluster

> Map of OECD AI Principles. Adopted 22 May 2019; revised May 2024. International voluntary principles for trustworthy AI. First intergovernmental AI standard. Influenced ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, multiple national AI strategies.

## Anchors

- [position](pillars/oecd-ai/position.md) · [anchors](pillars/oecd-ai/anchors.md)

## Provenance

- **OECD AI Expert Group** (AIGO) — established 2018.
- **OECD AI Principles** (Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence) — adopted 22 May 2019.
- **G20 AI Principles** (June 2019) — drew on OECD.
- **2024 update** — revised May 2024 in response to generative AI maturation. Addresses misinformation, mis/disinformation, sustainability.

## What OECD AI Principles are

International voluntary principles for trustworthy AI. Two parts:

- **Five values-based principles** for responsible AI stewardship.
- **Five recommendations** for national policies and international cooperation.

OECD member countries (38 countries) plus non-member adherents committed to implementing.

## Five values-based principles

1. **Inclusive growth, sustainable development, well-being**.
2. **Human-centred values and fairness** (incl. respect for rule of law, human rights, democratic values).
3. **Transparency and explainability**.
4. **Robustness, security and safety**.
5. **Accountability**.

Detail in [[OECD AI Values-Based Principles]].

## Five recommendations for national policies

6. **Investing in AI research and development**.
7. **Fostering a digital ecosystem for AI**.
8. **Shaping an enabling policy environment for AI**.
9. **Building human capacity and preparing for labour market transition**.
10. **International co-operation for trustworthy AI**.

## 2024 update changes

- **Mis/disinformation** addressed.
- **Information integrity** strengthened.
- **Sustainability** dimension expanded.
- **GenAI considerations** incorporated.

## Adherents

OECD members (38 countries) + non-member adherents (Argentina, Brazil, Costa Rica, Egypt, Malta, Peru, Romania, Singapore, Ukraine, and growing).

Total ~50 jurisdictions formally adherent.

## Why this matters

- **First international AI standard** influences subsequent frameworks (ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act).
- **Diplomatic baseline** for cross-jurisdictional AI governance.
- **National strategies reference**.
- **Procurement-readable** for international vendors.
