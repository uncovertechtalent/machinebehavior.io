> Primary documents, operating bodies, training providers, named practitioners, reference resources for the ITIL cluster.

## Primary documents (ITIL 4)

- **ITIL 4 Foundation** — entry-level core publication. Defines the SVS, SVC, four dimensions, seven guiding principles, basic practice descriptions.
- **ITIL 4 Create, Deliver and Support (CDS)** — MP-stream module publication.
- **ITIL 4 Drive Stakeholder Value (DSV)** — MP-stream module publication.
- **ITIL 4 High-Velocity IT (HVIT)** — MP-stream module publication. The Agile / DevOps reconciliation.
- **ITIL 4 Direct, Plan and Improve (DPI)** — shared MP / SL module publication.
- **ITIL 4 Digital and IT Strategy (DITS)** — SL-stream module publication.
- **ITIL 4 Practice Guides** — 34 individual guides, one per practice. Updated through 2023-2024 refresh.
- **ITIL 4 Specialist publications** — topic-specific (Sustainability, Acquiring and Managing Cloud Services, Business Relationship Management, others).

Publisher: PeopleCert (acquired AXELOS 2021). peoplecert.org.

## Primary documents (historical)

- **ITIL v3 / 2011 — Service Strategy, Service Design, Service Transition, Service Operation, Continual Service Improvement** — five core books. Still in use in v3-trained workforces.
- **ITIL v2 — Service Support, Service Delivery** — pre-2007 core. Historical reference.

## Owning bodies

- **PeopleCert** — current owner of ITIL IP since 2021. Cyprus-headquartered training and certification body, also owns PRINCE2, MSP, P3O, Lean IT, DevOps Institute IP, and several adjacent best-practice frameworks.
- **AXELOS** (defunct as independent entity) — owned ITIL from 2013-2021. Joint venture between UK Cabinet Office and Capita.
- **Office of Government Commerce (OGC)** (defunct) — owned ITIL from 2001-2013.
- **CCTA** (defunct) — UK Central Computer and Telecommunications Agency, originator of ITIL v1.

## Training and accreditation providers

PeopleCert delivers certification exams; training is delivered by Accredited Training Organizations (ATOs). Major ATOs:

- **GoodeLearning / GoGood**
- **Quint Wellington Redwood**
- **ITSM Academy**
- **Sage Computing / SACT**
- **Pink Elephant**
- **APMG-International** (formerly an accreditor; reduced role since PeopleCert consolidation)
- **G2G3 / Capgemini Academy**
- Many regional and language-specific ATOs

Self-study + PeopleCert direct exam booking is also widely used, particularly for Foundation.

## Tooling ecosystem (commonly cited in practitioner contexts)

ITIL is framework, not tool. Common tooling that shapes practice:

- **ServiceNow** — dominant enterprise ITSM platform. ITSM, ITOM, CSM modules. Opinionated ITIL implementation.
- **Jira Service Management** (Atlassian) — DevOps-adjacent ITSM. Lighter ITIL framing.
- **BMC Helix** — long-standing enterprise ITSM platform.
- **Ivanti Neurons / Ivanti Service Manager**
- **Cherwell** (acquired by Ivanti)
- **Freshservice** (Freshworks) — SMB-oriented.
- **SolarWinds Service Desk** — SMB-oriented.
- **ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus**
- **Zendesk** (more customer-support than IT-service-management, with ITSM extensions).

Tool selection shapes which practices receive deep implementation. ServiceNow's Configuration Management Database (CMDB) emphasis, for instance, drives Service Configuration Management practice depth in ServiceNow customers.

## Adjacent and competing frameworks

- **ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018** — service management management system standard. Certifiable. Direct ISO sibling to ITIL.
- **COBIT 2019** (ISACA) — IT governance framework. Complementary to ITIL; COBIT for governance, ITIL for management.
- **VeriSM** (2017-) — value-driven service management framework. Less adopted; saw activity 2018-2020.
- **MOF — Microsoft Operations Framework** — Microsoft-specific service management framework. Largely deprecated.
- **TOGAF** (The Open Group Architecture Framework) — enterprise architecture; complementary.
- **DevOps body of knowledge / DASA** — DevOps-specific framework; some practitioners use as ITIL alternative.
- **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)** — Google-origin; not a framework in the ITIL sense but a coherent body of practice with overlapping concerns.
- **Lean IT** — Lean principles applied to IT; complementary.
- **Six Sigma** — quality management; some IT applications.
- **Agile / Scrum / SAFe** — software development frameworks with operations implications.

## Named practitioners and reference voices

### ITIL 4 architects and contributors

- **AXELOS chief architects** — the team that led the 2019 ITIL 4 design. Less individually-attributed than v3 era; the architecture was institutional.
- **Mark Smalley** — long-standing ITIL contributor and trainer. Public commentary on framework evolution.
- **Stuart Rance** — practitioner-author with multiple ITIL-related publications.
- **Claire Agutter** — ITIL trainer and podcast host. Practitioner-accessible content.
- **Karen Ferris** — Australian ITSM voice; organizational-change focus.

### Adjacent / critical voices

- **Charles Betz** — IT industry analyst (formerly Forrester). Connects ITIL to enterprise architecture and DevOps. Wrote "Managing Digital."
- **Gene Kim** — DevOps community (Phoenix Project, DevOps Handbook). Adjacent to but not antagonistic toward ITIL; ITIL 4's HVIT module reflects DevOps community influence.
- **Niko Kaintantzis, Tony Price** — Practitioner authors on ITIL adoption.
- **Rob England** — "IT Skeptic" — long-running critical commentary on ITIL adoption pathologies. Influential on practitioner-side reform thinking.

### SRE community

- **Benjamin Treynor Sloss** — Google VP, originator of SRE. SRE book editor.
- **Niall Murphy, Chris Jones** — SRE book co-editors.
- **Liz Fong-Jones** — SRE / observability practitioner; cross-pollinator between SRE and broader operations community.

### Service-management academic / consulting

- **Peter Hill, Aidan Lawes** — itSMF (IT Service Management Forum) historical figures.
- **Various Big-Four service-management consulting partners** — published thought-leadership on ITIL adoption in enterprise contexts.

## Reference resources

- **peoplecert.org** — official body. Publication catalogue, certification scheme, exam booking.
- **itil.com** — informational, PeopleCert-controlled.
- **itSMF — IT Service Management Forum** — user-group membership organization. Country-level chapters (itSMF UK, itSMF International).
- **DevOps Institute** — PeopleCert-owned since 2022. Bridges DevOps and ITIL communities.
- **The ITSM Review** — practitioner publication.
- **Service Desk Institute** — research and benchmarking.

## Regulatory and procurement context

- **No regulatory mandate for ITIL.** Unlike ISO 27001 (regulatory-adjacent) or TISAX (sector-mandated), ITIL is voluntary.
- **Procurement procurement-readiness benefit.** Enterprise RFPs often ask for ITIL-trained workforce or ITIL-aligned operations. Specific certification levels sometimes specified for managed-service providers.
- **ISO/IEC 20000 certification** sometimes specified instead of or alongside ITIL adoption — particularly for managed-service providers serving regulated industries.
