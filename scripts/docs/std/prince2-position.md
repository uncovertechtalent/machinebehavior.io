## What it does well

- **Tailorable structure** — principles + processes + tailoring.
- **UK / EU public sector** recognition.
- **Stage-based governance** matches risk-managed project execution.
- **Roles and responsibilities** explicit.
- **PRINCE2 Agile** option for hybrid contexts.

## What it does poorly

- **Heavy on process** — bureaucratic implementation common.
- **Document-heavy artifact set.**
- **Tailoring discipline weak** in many implementations.
- **PMBOK dominance** in US limits PRINCE2 international reach.
- **CPD renewal** added cost since 2023.

## Evidence

- UK government project management standard.
- EU public sector partial adoption.
- Private sector adoption variable.
- PRINCE2 7 (2023) adoption ramping.

## Personal calibration

- For UK / EU public-sector engagements: PRINCE2 vocabulary load-bearing.
- For private-sector / US-aligned: PMBOK / Scrum-Agile more common.
