## Voluntary status

No enforcement mechanism:

- Adherent states implement voluntarily.
- Variance significant.
- Procurement signal weaker than regulations.

## High abstraction

Principles operate at ethical / values level:

- Operational implementation requires concrete frameworks.
- "We follow OECD AI" insufficient claim.
- Implementation depth varies dramatically.

## Geopolitical divergence

OECD adherence skewed Western:

- China not adherent.
- Russia not adherent.
- Some non-OECD jurisdictions adopting alternative frameworks.

## Update cadence

2019 → 2024 was substantial:

- Pre-2024 framing dated by GenAI maturation.
- Future major updates likely needed.

## Counterpoint

- Genuinely useful diplomatic baseline.
- Brussels-effect catalyst.
- Subsequent operational frameworks aligned.
- 2024 update kept current.
