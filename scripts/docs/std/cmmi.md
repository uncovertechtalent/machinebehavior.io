> Capability Maturity Model Integration. ISACA-owned (since 2017 acquisition from CMU SEI). Process maturity model with five maturity levels. CMMI 3.0 (2023) current. Origins in US DoD software acquisition; broad industry adoption.

## Anchors

- [position](pillars/cmmi/position.md) · [anchors](pillars/cmmi/anchors.md)

## Provenance

- **CMM** (1991) — Capability Maturity Model from Carnegie Mellon SEI. US DoD-funded.
- **CMMI** (2000+) — integration of multiple CMM variants.
- **CMMI v1.3** (2010), **CMMI v2.0** (2018).
- **ISACA acquired CMMI Institute** (2017) from CMU.
- **CMMI v3.0** (2023) — current. Modernized scope, agile-friendly, sustainability dimension.

## What CMMI is

Process maturity model with two views:

- **Maturity levels** (1-5) applied across the org.
- **Capability levels** applied per process area.

Process areas grouped:

- **Development**
- **Services**
- **Supplier management**

CMMI 3.0 expanded to include:

- **Cybersecurity**
- **Data management**
- **Workforce**
- **Safety**
- **Sustainability**

## Five maturity levels

1. **Initial** — ad hoc.
2. **Managed** — basic management.
3. **Defined** — standardized processes.
4. **Quantitatively Managed** — metrics-driven.
5. **Optimizing** — continuous improvement.

Detail in [[CMMI Maturity and Capability Levels]].

## CMMI vs ASPICE vs SPICE

All SPICE-derived process maturity models:

- **CMMI**: ISACA, broad industry, US-DoD lineage.
- **ASPICE**: VDA, automotive-specific.
- **SPICE (ISO/IEC 33000)**: ISO foundation.

Common SPICE roots; different specializations.

## Appraisal

CMMI Institute (ISACA) certified Lead Appraisers conduct formal appraisals:

- **SCAMPI** (Standard CMMI Appraisal Method for Process Improvement) — historical.
- **CMMI appraisals** under v2.0+ — streamlined.

Org achieves maturity level rating; valid 3 years.

## Why this matters

- **US DoD contractor requirement** historically.
- **Aerospace and defense** common.
- **Indian / offshore software services** for procurement signal.
- **Some financial services**.
- **CMMI 3.0 sustainability** dimension new.
