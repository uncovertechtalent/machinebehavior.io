## FedRAMP cost and timeline

- Authorization timeline 12-18 months.
- Cost $300k-1M+ for assessment.
- $200k-500k+ annual continuous monitoring.
- Smaller cloud providers gated.

## CMMC implementation lag

CMMC 1.0 announced 2020; CMMC 2.0 simplified; full rollout phased through 2028:

- Industry investment uncertainty.
- C3PAO capacity bottleneck.
- Smaller DoD contractor burden.

## 3PAO / C3PAO capacity

Limited certified assessor pool:

- Demand exceeds supply.
- Schedule pressure.
- Cost pressure.

## FedRAMP authorization concentration

JAB authorizations few; agency authorizations fragmented:

- Reciprocity imperfect.
- Multi-agency use cases complicated.

## CMMC scope ambiguity

CUI scoping challenges:

- What is CUI varies by agency.
- Contractor scope decisions consequential.
- Boundary uncertainty.

## Smaller-contractor burden

Both programs burden smaller orgs:

- CMMC Level 2 self-attestation reduces but doesn't eliminate.
- FedRAMP largely inaccessible to small SaaS.

## Counterpoint

- Genuine federal procurement security uplift.
- NIST 800-53 / 800-171 technical foundation strong.
- CMMC 2.0 proportional approach improvement over 1.0.
- FedRAMP continuous monitoring meaningful.
