# CRA Anchors

## Primary text

- **Regulation (EU) 2024/2847** — Cyber Resilience Act. Published OJEU 20 November 2024.

## Adjacent regulation

- **NIS2 (Dir 2022/2555)** — operator cybersecurity.
- **EU AI Act (Reg 2024/1689)** — high-risk AI products.
- **MDR (Reg 2017/745)** — medical devices (CRA excludes).
- **UN R155 / R156** — vehicles (CRA excludes).
- **Product Liability Directive (Dir 2024/2853)** — product liability including AI.

## Reference standards

- **EN 18031** — cybersecurity for radio equipment (RED Delegated Act).
- **ETSI EN 303 645** — consumer IoT cybersecurity baseline.
- **ISO/IEC 27034** — application security.
- **ISO/IEC 15408 (Common Criteria)** — IT product security evaluation.

## Operating bodies

- **European Commission** — DG CNECT.
- **ENISA** — implementation guidance.
- **Member State market surveillance authorities** — enforcement.
- **Notified Bodies** for third-party conformity assessment of important / critical products.
