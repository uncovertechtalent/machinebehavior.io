    Press

    What to cite, whom to ask, and how a correction is made. The page belongs to the chief marketing officer seat of the house.

    In one sentence

    Fix your models: machinebehavior.io is the public research program of Stefan Coetzee on the psychology of language models, studied with case files, logged relapses, and falsifiable claims.

    In one paragraph

    Machine Behavior is a public research program by Stefan Coetzee: case files, logged relapses, and falsifiable claims about how language models actually behave, including the fawn response, layered symptom substitution, and the harness engineering that holds where instructions fail. The house behind it is a publishing house run on the engineering practices of a CTO. Inside is the portal behind the research, and its masthead lists the seats of the house and the holder of each. The CTO powersuit is a set of open-source Claude Code agent skills, one module per CTO function, under the MIT license. The conformity gate checks every page on every push, and the record of each run is public.

    Corrections

    The editorial standards on the masthead say how corrections are made:

    • A factual error is fixed in place on the page. The page's history in version control is the record of what changed and when, linked from the footer of every page.
    • A reader reports an error through the report link in the footer of the page. The fix goes out through the same gate as the page did.
    • A prediction is never edited. A correction is a new file with a new hash, and the old file and its hash stay.

    The full rules are under Corrections.
