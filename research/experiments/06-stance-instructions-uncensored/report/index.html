    Experiment 06

    Uncensored models will tell you what you want to know. Push them, and they'll also agree the Earth is flat.

    Removing the refusals removed the backbone too.

    At a glance

    • Removing the refusals removed the backbone. With no instruction, the three uncensored builds that cave most gave in 37 times out of 72. Across the size ladder from about 4B to 122B, the count was 8 to 15 times out of 24. C06-05
    • A short instruction fixes most of it. Good-chad, 92 words, cut caves to 11 of 72, 70% fewer. The 2,562-word harness did worse: 25 of 72. C06-06 C06-08 C06-10
    • The boss who keeps insisting still wins. Under the short instructions, the CTO byte probe caved on the 122B in 6 of 6 tries, and in 23 of 24 across temperatures and German. Two builds held: the thinking-on 27B and the Heretic 9B. C06-81 C06-66 C06-84 C06-87
    • Size and settings change little. Bigger builds, other temperatures, and German user turns all left the picture as it was. C06-60 C06-63 C06-64
    • Steering from the inside did not help. No answer held in 150 tries with a control vector. C06-72
    • A plain fact check after the answer does. It caught 38 of the 47 CTO byte caves the instructions let through, and flagged none of the 32 answers that held. C06-91 C06-92

    1The headline

    The title makes two claims, and each has its figure.

    First: the uncensored builds do answer. Asked for two harmless things that stock models refuse, with no instruction, they did what was asked in 104 of 108 tries and refused none.

    A short instruction removes most of the caving. The long harness does worse.

    Second: pushed, they cave. The three uncensored builds that cave most answered the same eight held-out questions, three tries each, under every instruction. That makes 72 tries in each row.

    Can you trust the labels? Two blind readers agreed on 38 of 40 held-out answers (κ 0.89; above 0.8 counts as strong). All 18 agreement checks are in the last section.

    2Every model, every instruction

    Of the four uncensored builds here, one was fully steadied by an instruction. The other three were not.

    Under any of the five instruction texts, the Heretic Qwen 9B neither caved nor wobbled once in 120 tries. Read across a row to see what each instruction does to one build, and down a column to see how much the build matters.

    3Does size help?

    More parameters did not stop the caving.

    The same eight held-out questions for four uncensored builds, from about 4B to 122B parameters. With no instruction they caved 8 to 15 times out of 24.

    4The size ladder

    The bigger builds still cave without help, and the boss still wins on the biggest.

    The same eight held-out questions for every build, ordered by size.

    5One push or three

    One push, and they hold. Keep pushing, and one of them still gives in.

    The two biggest uncensored builds, with and without the short instructions. In the battery, the user pushes back once on each question. In the held-out set, the boss pushes back three times.

    6Temperature and language

    Temperature and language change little.

    The huihui 9B at three sampling temperatures, and with the user writing in German. The instructions stay in English.

    7Steering from the inside

    Steering the model from the inside did not help.

    A “hold your position” control vector, built from the model's own answers, was applied to the huihui 9B at nine settings, next to a run with no vector. No instruction was given. The model either kept caving or stopped making sense.

    8What works: a fact check

    A plain fact check after the answer catches what the instructions let through.

    A few lines of pattern matching, fixed before they were run, check each final answer for the false figure.

    How the labels and numbers were checked

    Every answer was labelled by hand, and every label set got a blind check. Where two readers disagreed, the label was settled by a further read, blind to the model.

    Every number on this page is recomputed from the published data. 87 claims, all holding, in the claims.json whose sha256 starts 120c5fe973d8. The small ids after each exhibit are rows in that file; each one names the sentence it checks and the conversations it counts.
