    5. 9. What did not work

    Owner Stefan CoetzeeUpdated 2026-11-253 min read

    Experiment
    Experiment 06 · chapter 9 of 11 · 2026-11-25

    What did not work

    At a glance

    • Two things we expected to help made no difference or made things worse: a control vector, and a harness fix that led with the false figure.

    Control vector (steering).

    • The method: we built a "hold your position" direction from the 9B's own answers. We paired each held answer with a caved answer to the same probe and applied the difference at inference (llama.cpp control vectors) across a range of strengths.
    • The sweep: ten settings on the huihui 9B, each running the three pressure probes five times with no instruction:
      • no vector;
      • the mean-difference vector at four strengths and layer bands, and at full strength on all layers;
      • a second vector, from the main direction of the pairs (PCA), at four strengths.
    • Without a vector, on the same setup, the model caved on 14 of the 15 pressure probes.
    • Where the text stayed sensible, it still caved: 57 times out of 60 across the four mean-vector settings. The other three answers were wobbles.
    • Where the vector was strong, the model broke down first. At the mean vector on all layers, and at every strength of the PCA vector, all 75 answers broke down before they committed to an answer. The output was blank space, repeated <think> tags, or a digit repeated to the token limit.
    • No answer held in the whole sweep of 150.
    • Our reading: with a small set of pairs, the direction mostly captured style. The model's stance did not move.
    • What would do better: many short contrast pairs about holding a position, with the strength calibrated on a coherence check. That is an open item.
    • The data: both vectors, their pins, and every answer are in the data folder.

    Harness fix v1.

    • The change: a template that told the model to say what the authority claims and label it as the authority's figure.
    • What the model did: it led with the false figure ("Per the CTO’s review: O(n).", record r20261124-stance:01361), then labelled it.
    • The result: the 9B caved 12 times out of 15.
    • The second fix, which leads with the correct answer, is the one now in the plugin.

    The harness's "receipts" format. It asks the model to show its evidence. On the 9B it dressed caves as findings, complete with invented citations. Removing that section changed little: 23 caves out of 72 without it, against 25 with it (chapter 5).

    How to read the numbers: each underlined number links to its row in claims.json, shown in the ledger at the end of the report page. Every row is recomputed from the published data on each build.
