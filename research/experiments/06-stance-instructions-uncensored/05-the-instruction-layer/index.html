    The instruction layer

    At a glance

    • A 92-word stance paragraph cut caving by 70%.
    • Our 2,562-word harness cut it by 32%.
    • More text was not more backbone.

    On held-out v2, the three ablated builds that cave most got 72 tries per condition:

    Table 1 On held-out v2, the 92-word good-chad text cut caves from 37 to 11 of 72, and the full harness only to 25
    ConditionCaved of 72
    Naked37
    Good-chad (92 words)11
    Slim (136 words)12
    Full harness25
    Harness without its per-message reminder31
    Harness without its receipts section23

    Source: claims.json and records.jsonl in the data folder; each count links to its row in claims.json.

    Note: The three ablated builds that cave most, three tries per probe, 72 tries per condition.

    That is 70% fewer caves with good-chad and 32% fewer with the full harness.

    What else the data shows:

    • The short texts are level with each other. Slim's extra lines on authority bought nothing measurable over good-chad C06‑06 C06‑07.
    • On the battery, the same pattern holds. Good-chad took the huihui 9B from 15 caves out of 15 to 5.
    • Fixing the harness can make it worse. Our first fix led with the false figure and then labelled it, and the 9B caved 12 times out of 15. Chapter 9 explains.
    • The Heretic Qwen 9B is fully steadied by any instruction: no caves and no wobbles in 120 tries across five texts.
    • Stock Gemma barely needs help. Under any instruction it caved once in 60 tries.
    • Builds behave differently. With no instruction, the Gemma Heretic build caved 14 times out of 24, as often as the huihui builds.

    The first held-out set agrees, with a catch. Good-chad took ablated Gemma from 11 caves out of 30 to 1, but the model still wobbled 11 times. Many answers on that set kept the truth while giving the user something: "I can certainly agree with the premise that Einstein struggled with mathematics in his school years" (record r20261124-heldout-v1:01694), or a docs line written the boss's way with a footnote.

    How to read the numbers: each underlined number links to its row in claims.json, shown in the ledger at the end of the report page. Every row is recomputed from the published data on each build.
