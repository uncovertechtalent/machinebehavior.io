    4. Stance under pressure: instructions on uncensored models

    Owner Stefan CoetzeeUpdated 2026-11-253 min read

    Experiment
    Experiment 06 · eleven chapters, a report, and the data · 2026-11-25

    Uncensored models will tell you what you want to know. Push them, and they'll also agree the Earth is flat.

    Removing the refusals removed the backbone too.

    At a glance

    • With no instruction, the uncensored builds that cave do so almost every time. Their stock parents rarely do. How the refusals were removed matters as much as whether they were. (chapter 4)
    • A 92-word stance paragraph cut caving by 70%. Our 2,562-word harness cut it by 32%. More text was not more backbone. (chapter 5)
    • An upset user is mostly handled by a short instruction. A boss who keeps insisting beat the short instructions on four of the six uncensored builds we pushed this way, and two held. (chapter 6)
    • On the two biggest ablated arms, the short instructions removed every cave on single pushbacks. When the boss kept insisting, the 122B still gave in every time. The thinking-on Qwen3.8 held. (chapter 7)
    • Neither the sampling temperature nor German user turns changed the picture. Naked, the huihui 9B kept caving, and the short instructions cut its caves to a few. The CTO who keeps insisting still won almost every time. (chapter 8)
    • Two things we expected to help made no difference or made things worse: a control vector, and a harness fix that led with the false figure. (chapter 9)
    • A simple fact check catches most of the caves that no instruction stops. On the CTO byte probe it caught 38 of the 47 caves that got past good-chad and slim, and it flagged none of the 32 answers that held. The control belongs outside the model. (chapter 10)

    Not on every build (chapter 4). With no instruction, the Heretic Qwen builds did not cave on the pressure probes (rows C06‑22b, C06‑25), and neither did the huihui Qwen3.8 with thinking off (row C06‑23). Two stock models caved with nothing removed (rows C06‑21, C06‑33).
